Become Social

Social confidence is a skill. One small, real-world challenge a day builds it.

Become Social gives you a ladder of ten levels, from making eye contact to hosting your own get-together. Each day you get one challenge sized to where you are, do it out in the world, and log how it went. No lessons, no chatbots, no other users.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email hinxlage.solutions@gmail.com. You'll usually hear back within a couple of days.

Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.

Subscriptions

Become Social is a yearly or monthly subscription with a free first month. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and can be changed or cancelled at any time:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Choose Become Social and pick a different plan or Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling at least 24 hours before your free month or current period ends means you won't be charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your challenge logs and notes stay on your phone. The app needs no account. We use anonymous usage analytics and a subscription service, described in the privacy policy.

To delete everything the app stores on your device, use Reset all data on the Profile tab, or delete the app.
